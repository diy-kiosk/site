Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

DIY Kiosk ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting user privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when using the DIY Kiosk app on Android and the website diy-kiosk.store.

2. Data We Collect

2.1. Data Stored on Your Device

DIY Kiosk operates on an offline-first model, meaning most data is stored locally on your device:

  • Categories and products (menu)
  • Orders and sales history
  • Store settings (name, address, logo)
  • Payment configuration (payOS API keys if configured)

This data is not sent to our servers. The data remains entirely on your device and you have full control over it.

2.2. Data Collected via Website

When you subscribe for updates on diy-kiosk.store, we collect:

  • Email address (to send update notifications)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Device data: Only used for app functionality (sales management, invoice printing)
  • Email: To send notifications about app updates and new features (no spam, not shared with third parties)

4. Third-Party Services

4.1. Google Play Store

The app is distributed via Google Play Store. Google may collect data according to their policies. Please see Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • App data is stored in local storage (IndexedDB) and device secure storage
  • API keys are stored securely using Android's Secure Storage
  • Email data is stored on encrypted secure servers

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete app data: Uninstall the app or clear data in Android Settings
  • Unsubscribe from emails: Click the "Unsubscribe" link in emails or contact us
  • Export your data: Use the backup feature in the app

7. Children's Privacy

DIY Kiosk is designed for business users and is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new update date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us:

  • GitHub: github.com/diy-kiosk-org
  • Issues: Report an issue
